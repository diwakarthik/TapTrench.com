How it works

Three steps. Zero fuss.

You send us your link. We program it into your plate. Your customers just tap.

01

Copy your review link

In your Google Business Profile, tap “Get more reviews” and copy the link it gives you.

02

We program and lock it

Send it to us when you activate. We program it into your plate's secure chip and lock it in.

03

Customers tap, you get reviews

Any iPhone or Android phone opens your review page with one tap. Unlimited taps, forever.

No more awkward asking

The plate does the asking. Your team just points to it with a smile.
