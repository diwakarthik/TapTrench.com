  2. How it works

Simple for you. Simpler for your customers.

You don't program anything yourself. Send us your link, our team sets up your plate and locks it in, and from then on your customers just tap.

  1. Order your plate

    Pick a review plate, a menu plate or both from the shop.

  2. Copy your Google review link

    Open your Google Business Profile, tap “Get more reviews” and copy the link it gives you. For a menu plate, copy the link to your online menu or ordering page.

    Google Business ProfileGet more reviewsCopy link

  3. Send it to us

    Share the link on our Activate your product page along with your order details.

  4. We program and lock it

    An authorised Tap Trench technician programs your link into the plate's high-security chip and locks it, so it can't be changed or tampered with.

  5. Customers tap. Forever.

    Place the plate on your counter or table. Every tap opens your page instantly, with no limit and no monthly fee.

For your customers

One tap. Straight to your review page.

When a customer taps your review plate, your Google review page opens immediately. They choose their stars, write their review in their own words and post it.

  • No app to download
  • No searching for your business on Google
  • Works on iPhone and Android with our advanced NFC chips
Built to last

Set once. Works forever.

Locked for security

Your link is locked into a high-security chip, so nobody can redirect your customers somewhere else.

Unlimited taps

There's no tap limit and no expiry. Use it as many times as your customers like.

No subscription

Many major players charge a monthly fee. You pay once for a Tap Trench plate.

Built for life

No battery to run flat and nothing to charge. The chip is sealed under a tough epoxy dome.
